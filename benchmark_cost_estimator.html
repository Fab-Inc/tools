Language Doc tokens Gen total Eval total Cost (per model)
Reasoning tokens are billed as output tokens by major providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek). Per-criterion mode duplicates document tokens across every criterion call; batched mode sends each document once but returns all verdicts in a single longer response. Token-per-word ratios for African languages are approximate and depend heavily on the specific tokenizer.
